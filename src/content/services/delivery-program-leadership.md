I've spent most of my career leading complex programs, and a good part of it recovering programs that had gone red. I run programs on fundamentals: agreed success criteria up front, honest governance, early escalation, and executive communication that lands. When a program is in trouble, I find the real problem, rebuild trust, and put a plan in place that people believe.
